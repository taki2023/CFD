About

Torvex Lab is designed and developed by Taki Eddine Goumri.

Taki Eddine Goumri

Mechanical engineer working on fluid mechanics, heat transfer and CFD.

I trained as a mechanical engineer at the École Nationale Polytechnique of Algiers and completed a Master 2 in energy conversion machines at ENSAM. My work has moved steadily towards numerical simulation: understanding the assumptions behind a model, how it behaves numerically, and how far its results can be trusted against reference cases.

Torvex Lab started as a way to understand a CFD code from the inside — geometry, meshing, solver and post-processing — and grew into a complete 2-D and 3-D workbench.
