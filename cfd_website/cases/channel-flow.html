Problem

Fluid enters a long channel of height H with a uniform velocity Ū. Friction at the walls slows the fluid near them and speeds up the core until, after an entrance length, the profile stops changing. The fully developed flow has an exact solution, so the error of the solver can be measured, not just estimated.
