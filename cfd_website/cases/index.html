Case studies

Each study sets up a classic problem in TakiCFD — geometry, mesh, boundary conditions and solver settings — and compares the results with experiments, published numerical benchmarks or exact solutions.

  • Lid-driven cavity

    The classic first test of an incompressible solver: a square box whose lid slides, driving one large vortex.

    Compared with: Ghia, Ghia & Shin (1982)

  • Flow over a cylinder

    A steady wake with two attached vortices at Re = 20 and 40, and periodic vortex shedding at Re = 100.

    Compared with: Dennis & Chang (1970), Williamson (1996)

  • Counter-flow heat exchanger

    Two streams separated by a conducting wall: conjugate heat transfer compared with ε-NTU theory and laminar Nusselt numbers.

    Compared with: Shah & London (1978), Incropera et al.

  • Laminar channel flow

    Flow developing between two plates into the exact parabolic Poiseuille profile — the simplest check of the viscous terms.

    Compared with: Analytic (Poiseuille)
