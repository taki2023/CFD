Problem

A hot stream and a cold stream flow in opposite directions through two parallel channels separated by a solid plate. Heat passes from the hot fluid through the plate into the cold fluid. The outlet temperatures depend on how much heat the exchanger can pass compared with the heat capacity of the streams — the effectiveness–NTU relation of heat-exchanger theory.

The case exercises the energy equation, the coupling between fluid and solid cells (conjugate heat transfer) and the thermal boundary layers. The local Nusselt number in the fully developed part of each channel is also compared with the exact laminar values.

Comparison with reference data

The effectiveness of a counter-flow exchanger is

ε = [1 − exp(−NTU (1 − Cr))] / [1 − Cr exp(−NTU (1 − Cr))],  ε = NTU / (1 + NTU) for Cr = 1

with NTU = UA / Cmin and 1/U = 1/hh + t/kw + 1/hc. From the simulation, ε is measured directly from the outlet temperatures: ε = Ch(Th,in − Th,out) / [Cmin(Th,in − Tc,in)].
